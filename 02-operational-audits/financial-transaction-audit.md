
# Operational Audit: Financial Transaction Review

## 1. Audit Objective
Determine whether financial transactions are
reviewed, discrepancies are investigated, and
appropriate documentation is maintained.

## 2. Identified Risk
Unauthorized or fraudulent transactions may go
undetected, potentially resulting in financial
losses or inaccurate reporting.

## 3. Existing Control
Management reviews daily transaction reports
and investigates identified discrepancies.

## 4. NIST 800-53 Mapping
- Control: AU-6
- Family: Audit and Accountability
- Control Name: Audit Record Review, Analysis,
  and Reporting

## 5. Evidence Requested
- Daily transaction reports
- Exception reports
- Investigation documentation
- Management sign-offs
- Resolution timestamps

## 6. Testing Methodology
Select 10 transaction discrepancies and verify
that each was investigated and documented.

## 7. Sample Findings
2 of 10 sampled discrepancies lacked
documented investigation records.

## 8. Risk Impact
Undetected fraudulent transactions could result
in financial loss and unreliable reporting.

## 9. Recommendation
Implement a standardized discrepancy review
process requiring investigation notes,
resolution dates, and management approval.

## 10. Conclusion
The control requires improvement because
documentation was insufficient to demonstrate
consistent operation.
