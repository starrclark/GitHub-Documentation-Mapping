
## Audit Finding: Inconsistent Transaction Investigation

### Control Tested
AU-6 - Audit Record Review, Analysis, and Reporting

### Testing Results
10 transaction exceptions were sampled.

- 6 transactions were properly investigated.
- 2 transactions were reviewed but lacked
  documented investigations.
- 2 transactions had no documented review.

### Finding
4 out of 10 sampled transactions (40%) failed
to demonstrate compliance with the company's
transaction review and investigation procedure.

Two exceptions had evidence of review but lacked
investigation documentation. Another two lacked
documented evidence of review.

### Potential Business Impact
Inadequate transaction monitoring and investigation
could allow fraudulent activity, unauthorized
refunds or voids, and inaccurate financial reporting
to go undetected.

### Recommendation
Implement a standardized transaction investigation
procedure requiring managers to document:

- Reason for the transaction discrepancy.
- Investigation findings.
- Supporting evidence.
- Resolution and escalation details.
- Manager approval and completion date.

### Conclusion
The control is not operating consistently based
on the sample tested. Management should address
the identified documentation and investigation
weaknesses and perform follow-up testing.
